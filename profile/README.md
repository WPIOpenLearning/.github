## WPI Open Learning Platform

An innovate open learning platform for WPI bridges the gap between LMS and open access, allowing faculty to seamlessly share course materials from Canvas, the university's LMS. Check it out at [openlearning.wpi.edu](https://openlearning.wpi.edu).

Have questions, or interested in using the platform at your institution? [Contact us](mailto:gr-openlearning@wpi.edu).
